Refund & Editing Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Quvo builds a custom digital wedding invitation from the design you choose. All work is done after you send names, dates, photos, venue, and other details on WhatsApp.

Edits

  • We revise the invitation if it does not match the chosen design or the details you sent.
  • Standard orders include revisions until the invite matches those details.
  • Send edit requests on WhatsApp to +91 89039 62685 with a clear note of what to change.

Refunds

  • If you are still unhappy after one revision, you can ask for a full refund within 7 days of delivery.
  • Refunds are not given after the invitation link has been widely shared with guests, except where required by law.
  • Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.

What we need from you

Timelines start after we receive complete details and files. Incomplete photos or venue information can delay delivery.
