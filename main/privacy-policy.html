Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Quvo uses your details only to create and support your wedding invitation.

What we collect

  • WhatsApp number and messages you send us

How we use it

  • To customise and host your invitation link
  • To reply on WhatsApp about your order
  • To improve the gallery on phones

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Photos and guest-facing details appear on the invitation link you choose to share. Payment apps process money on their own systems if you pay that way.

Retention

Order chats and files are kept while the invitation is live, then removed on request after the event unless we must keep a record of the sale.
